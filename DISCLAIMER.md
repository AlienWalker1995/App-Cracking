# Intended use and limits

App-Cracker is reverse-engineering tooling for security research, interoperability
research, and competitive and educational analysis. It is published as tooling only.

## Use it only on apps you have the right to analyze

Reverse engineering is restricted in some jurisdictions and by some software licenses and
terms of service. You are responsible for ensuring you have the legal right to analyze a
given application before you do. Use this on your own apps, on apps you are authorized to
assess, or where local law permits study and interoperability research.

## What this repository does not contain

- No decrypted or extracted content from any third-party application: no assets, no
  bytecode dumps, no databases, no captured network traffic.
- No decryption keys, signing secrets, or per-target offsets.
- No modified or repackaged third-party applications.

Per-target keys and decoders are kept in a private workspace, never here.

## What it will not help you do

This is for understanding how software works. It is not for pirating paid apps, cheating
against other players, forging entitlements, or attacking live services you do not control.
